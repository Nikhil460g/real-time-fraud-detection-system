# Audit Strategy

## Overview

Audit processes ensure transparency, accountability, and compliance throughout the fraud detection platform.

## Audit Areas

### Security Audits

- Access reviews
- Authentication reviews
- Security policy verification

### Operational Audits

- Incident reviews
- Change management audits
- Backup verification

### Compliance Audits

- Regulatory compliance checks
- Data protection verification

## Audit Frequency

- Monthly operational audits
- Quarterly security audits
- Annual compliance audits

## Benefits

- Improved accountability
- Better compliance tracking
- Risk reduction
