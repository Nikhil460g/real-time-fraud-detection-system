# Business Continuity Plan

## Overview

This plan ensures that critical fraud detection operations continue during disruptions.

## Objectives

- Maintain service availability
- Protect customer transactions
- Minimize downtime
- Ensure rapid recovery

## Continuity Measures

- Multi-region deployment
- Automated failover
- Backup systems
- Disaster recovery procedures

## Recovery Priorities

1. Transaction Processing
2. Fraud Detection Engine
3. Risk Assessment Services
4. Case Management Services

## Benefits

- Reduced business disruption
- Improved resilience
- Faster recovery
